# MetaBotism Roadmap - Study Notes

## What the supplied PDF establishes

- Product promise: clearer, safer, measurable adherence process; no guaranteed semen or conception outcome.
- Primary format: private Android APK with WhatsApp-like interface. Revised decision: WhatsApp Business integration starts in parallel from day one, but APK remains primary and WhatsApp is only a channel adapter.
- Core architecture: deterministic versioned plan plus constrained AI coaching layer.
- Delivery order: discovery, prototype, offline APK, VPS/AI, pilot, then scale.
- Safety: no diagnosis, medication changes, Nitrix restart/dosing, tobacco, guaranteed claims, or autonomous checkout.
- Data: explicit consent, least collection, encryption, audit logs, export/delete, and recovery testing.

## Important clinical/product interpretation captured

Raw and post-preparation semen metrics must not be compared as if identical. A semen analysis alone is not a complete fertility verdict. Morphology is not a prediction of a future child's disability. Supplement decisions belong to a licensed clinician.

## Engineering conclusion

The PDF is strong as a product/safety brief but too broad for a first build if every listed integration is treated as MVP. The minimal correct product is an offline private adherence APK, with VPS and AI added only after local behavior is proven.

## Source limitations

The PDF's bilingual Urdu text extracts with broken font encoding in this environment. English executive handoff pages 30-46 were readable and used for implementation capture; rendered PDF pages should be visually reviewed before any disputed Urdu wording is treated as canonical.
