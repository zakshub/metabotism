# MetaBotism - Product Brief

## Short understanding

MetaBotism is a private, Urdu-first, right-to-left adherence coach for Faraz. It turns an approved nutrition, lifestyle, and follow-up-test plan into the next clear action, reduces shopping/cooking friction, records adherence, and prepares a comparable follow-up semen analysis.

It is not a fertility guarantee, doctor, diagnosis engine, supplement prescriber, or autonomous shopping bot.

## Recommended product shape

1. Private Android APK with a WhatsApp-like interface.
2. Offline-first core: schedule, meals, substitutions, groceries, logs, score, countdown, and safety templates.
3. WhatsApp Business integration starts from day one as a parallel channel, while the APK remains primary and reliable.
4. VPS: encrypted sync, authentication, backups, reports, AI coaching, and the WhatsApp channel adapter.
5. AI is a constrained explanation/coaching layer over versioned rules; it never owns clinical truth.
6. Single-user UX first, multi-user-ready data model second.

## MVP outcome

Faraz can install the APK, follow the plan without internet, respond to reminders, handle unavailable foods safely, see progress, and export/delete his data. The app must improve process adherence, not promise a biological result.

## Product principles

- Urdu-first, concise, actionable, respectful, firm but non-shaming.
- Deterministic rules for health, meals, quantities, test preparation, and prohibited actions.
- Minimum necessary sensitive data, explicit consent, private-by-default operation.
- Every high-risk or uncertain answer escalates instead of guessing.
- Build for product scale through stable IDs, versioned plans, auditability, and replaceable integrations.
