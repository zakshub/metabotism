# MetaBotism — complete 36-screen product flow

This is the expanded product scope based on the user-provided flow definition. The three supplied visual references are treated as anchor screens, not the complete product.

## Flow 1 — Entry & Authentication (5)

1. Splash screen
2. Welcome screen
3. Sign up
4. Login
5. Forgot/reset password

## Flow 2 — Onboarding & Health Profile (6)

6. Basic profile
7. Health background
8. Lifestyle assessment
9. Fertility goals
10. Dietary preferences
11. Onboarding summary and consent

## Flow 3 — Home Dashboard (4)

12. Today dashboard
13. Daily progress details
14. Weekly overview
15. Notifications centre

## Flow 4 — Daily Nutrition Plan (6)

16. Today’s meal plan
17. Meal details and benefits
18. Food replacement options
19. Grocery list
20. Grocery item details
21. Weekly meal calendar

## Flow 5 — Lifestyle & Habit Tracking (4)

22. Daily habits
23. Water and walking tracker
24. Heat-exposure tracker
25. Sleep and recovery tracker

## Flow 6 — Reports & Progress (5)

26. Reports library
27. Upload new report
28. Report processing
29. Report analysis
30. Progress comparison

The comparison model must support April 2025 and July 2026 values for concentration/count, motility, rapid linear and morphology. Values are context, not diagnosis.

## Flow 7 — AI Coach (3)

31. Coach conversation
32. Daily check-in
33. Weekly review and motivation

Coach tone can be firm or gentle but must remain safe. It must never recommend cigarette, naswar or tobacco as a reward. If use is reported, it should support reduction or cessation and escalate appropriately.

## Flow 8 — Account & Administration (3)

34. Profile and preferences
35. Privacy, consent and data
36. Settings and logout

## Delivery rule

Each screen will be a real navigable state with loading, empty, error and success states where relevant. The local MVP will work without an API key. Authentication, cloud persistence, report processing and AI responses remain capability-gated until their backend services are enabled.
